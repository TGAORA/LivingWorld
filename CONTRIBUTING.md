# Contribution

> *Contribution is participation in a world — not extraction from a project.*

This document defines how to contribute to **Living World MMO**, what is expected, how work is recognized, and how compensation and charity assignment function.

It is intentionally values-forward. Technical specifics may evolve; principles do not. Let's build something fun, safe, pure, and beneficial.

---

## 1. Who This Is For

You should consider contributing if you:

* Care about systemic simulation and emergence
* Are comfortable working without visible stats, meters, or shortcuts
* Value consequence over optimization
* Are interested in teaching as well as building

You should **not** contribute if you are seeking:

* Rapid monetization
* Ownership leverage
* Design-by-vote authority
* Power over the project’s direction

---

## 2. Contribution Types

Contributions may include (but are not limited to):

* Code (engine, simulation, tools, backend)
* Art assets (tools, weapons, armor, buildings, environments)
* Animation & sound
* Writing (narrative archives, world texts, cultural elements)
* Documentation & diagrams
* Prototyping & technical exploration

All contributions are evaluated within the context of the project’s **design rules**.

---

## 3. Design Boundaries (Non-Negotiable)

No contribution may:

* Introduce pay-to-win mechanics
* Expose hidden simulation layers
* Add safety systems that negate consequence
* Replace emergence with scripted outcomes
* Depend on global balance or static metas
* Be beyond PG-13 acceptance, we are creating a game, not something to corrupt youth

---

## 4. Contribution Records (Knowledge Logs)

Living World MMO uses **contribution records**, not surveillance or time tracking.

Primary method:

* A personal YouTube channel (public or private)

These videos document work and teach craft. Contributors retain full ownership and may monetize independently.

---

## 5. Time × Value Recognition

Compensation considers **both** time spent and value delivered. Low-impact work is valid but weighted lower.

---

## 6. Quality Validation

Validation includes:

1. Community signal
2. Studio review
3. (Future) AI-assisted filtering

Community feedback is never the sole authority.

---

## 7. Attribution & Anonymity

Options:

* Public
* Pseudonymous
* Anonymous (with compensation assigned to a charity)

Internal attribution is preserved for accountability.

---

## 8. Compensation & Charity Assignment

Contributors may receive compensation directly or assign it to supported charities, including the project-affiliated nonprofit supporting long-term coma and life-support care.

---

## 9. DLC & Ongoing Work

DLC focuses on expression, not power: visual styles, cultural motifs, and narrative archives.

---

## 10. Final Notes

This project is slow by design and values consequence, clarity, and memory.
