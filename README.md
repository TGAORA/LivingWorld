# Living World MMO

> **With every birth, a new path is forged.**
> **Death is never the end — only the beginning.**

Living World MMO is a **persistent world-experience MMO** built around consequence, memory, emergence, and collapse. This repository represents the canonical home of a project whose goal is not to optimize play, but to **simulate a world that remembers people**.

This README replaces all previous versions and reflects the current, authoritative direction of the project.

---

## What This Game Is

Living World MMO is a simulation-first online world where:

* The world exists whether players are online or not
* Progress is uneven, fragile, and reversible
* Collapse is systemic, not a failure state
* Death changes your role, not your value

Players begin as a single pre-historical human and may one day lead others, shape land, or influence culture — but nothing is permanent. The world continues without you.

---

## What This Game Is Not

This is **not** a traditional MMO.

There are:

* No levels
* No classes
* No tech trees
* No safe zones
* No PvP toggles
* No stat-driven combat
* No guaranteed progress

If you are looking for fairness, safety, or power fantasy, this project is not designed for you.

---

## Core Design Principles

* **Persistence over progress** – the world outlives individuals
* **Consequence over balance** – fairness is not guaranteed
* **Emergence over scripting** – systems create stories
* **Human scale first** – one person matters before armies
* **Hidden depth** – critical systems exist without meters or labels
* **Memory as mechanics** – history shapes future play

These principles are enforced across all design and technical decisions.

---

## Death, Continuity, and Meaning

Death in Living World MMO is not a reset.

When a player dies:

* Player control ends
* The character persists as a PvE individual
* Skills, reputation, trauma, and memory remain active in the world
* The player is reborn as a new pre-historical human

The world accumulates the echoes of those who lived in it.

---

## Tools, Weapons, and Armor

Weapons and armor are **tools used in violent contexts**, not separate systems.

* Tools emerge from materials, environment, and need
* Weapons provide leverage, not damage
* Armor buys time, not immunity
* All tools degrade, fail, and signal identity

There are no early research trees. Knowledge spreads through survival, imitation, and memory.

---

## Combat Philosophy

Combat is not about output.

It resolves against:

* Fatigue
* Balance
* Awareness
* Morale
* Bodily integrity

Most fights end in retreat, injury, or collapse — not death. Violence is costly, remembered, and often avoidable.

## Beyond the Game

Living World MMO is built on the belief that **existence has value even when agency is limited**.

As part of that belief:

* A large portion of game revenue supports established charities such as **St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital**
* The project also supports a project-affiliated nonprofit focused on individuals in long-term coma or reduced-consciousness states, assisting with extended life-support care and family support

This charitable mission is:

* Transparent
* Optional
* Entirely separate from gameplay systems

The world remembers people.
We believe the real one should too.
